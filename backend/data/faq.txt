## Section: Product Overview

Q: What is Geometra, in one line?
A: Geometra is an image-to-CAD system that allows you to measure your space, such as wall elevations, directly from photos taken with your phone by following the system's provided instructions.

Q: How does it actually work — step by step?
A: Place Marker: Put the Geometra marker on any surface to create a reference point. Take Photo: Take a photo where you can clearly see the wall edges and at least three corners. Measure: Instantly measure the wall through Geometra's interface, supported by robust backend processing and precise accuracy.

## Section: Hardware Requirements

Q: Do I need any special hardware, scanner, or laser device?
A: Printing the Marker: A laser printer is preferred. Be sure to follow the instructions, keep in portrait mode and maintain a custom scaling of 100%. Taking Photos: No specialized camera or high-end smartphone is required; any standard phone will work. Geometra Interface: Since Geometra is a web-based application, it can be used on any standard PC, laptop, or desktop computer.

## Section: Web App

Q: Do I have to download an app, or does it work in a browser?
A: Geometra functions completely as a web-based application without any necessary downloads, allowing it to be utilized on any standard desktop computer, laptop, or PC.

## Section: Output Formats

Q: What do I get at the end — what does the output look like?
A: Wall elevations are provided in both PDF and DXF formats. Image measurements are delivered in PNG and PDF formats.

Q: What happens if a measurement is wrong — can I verify it?
